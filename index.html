📖 Game Guide & FAQ

What is XO Arena and who developed it?

XO Arena is an advanced Progressive Web App (PWA) designed and developed by Y Mani Sasank Reddy. It modernizes the classic game of Tic Tac Toe with fast WebSocket multiplayer, three levels of Minimax AI, synthesized audio effects, and an innovative 3-piece Infinity mode.

How does Infinity Mode eliminate boring ties?

In standard 3x3 Tic Tac Toe, two skilled players will almost always end in a draw. Infinity Mode solves this: each player may only hold 3 marks simultaneously. When you place a 4th mark, your oldest mark fades away with an alert pulse, creating a fluid and deeply strategic battle.

How does the Minimax AI think?

The Master AI uses the recursive Minimax game-theory algorithm. It explores the entire decision tree of remaining moves, guaranteeing mathematically optimal moves on every turn.

How do online private rooms work?

Online multiplayer runs over secure WebSocket channels. When you create a private room, a unique 6-character code is generated. Your opponent enters the code or opens your direct invite link to connect peer-to-peer with zero server lag.
