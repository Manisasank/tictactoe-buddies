💡 Tactical Playbook & Strategy Tips

1. The Infinity Corner Trap
In Infinity Mode, occupy opposite corners. When your oldest mark is about to decay, place your 4th mark to create an unavoidable two-way fork.
2. Countering Tactical AI
Medium AI prioritizes blocking immediate 3-in-a-row threats. Bait its blocks to force openings along diagonal axes.
3. First-Move Dynamics
In Classic mode, taking the center gives optimal control. In Infinity mode, corners allow better rotation control over which marks decay first.

📖 Game Guide & FAQ

What is XO Arena and who developed it?

XO Arena is an advanced Progressive Web App (PWA) designed and developed by Y Mani Sasank Reddy, an AI and software engineer. It modernizes the classic game of Tic Tac Toe with fast WebSocket multiplayer, three levels of Minimax AI, synthesized audio effects, a floating glass capsule dock, and the innovative 3-Mark Dynamic Infinity Matrix™.

How does the Dynamic Infinity Matrix™ eliminate boring ties?

In standard 3x3 Tic Tac Toe, two skilled players will almost always end in a draw. Infinity Mode solves this: each player may only hold 3 marks simultaneously. When you place a 4th mark, your oldest mark fades away with an alert pulse, creating a fluid, deeply strategic battle with 100% decisive finishes.

How does the Minimax AI think?

The Master AI uses the recursive Minimax game-theory algorithm. It explores the entire decision tree of remaining moves, guaranteeing mathematically optimal moves on every turn.

How do online private rooms work?

Online multiplayer runs over secure WebSocket channels on HiveMQ (port 8884). When you create a private room, a unique 6-character code is generated. Your opponent enters the code or opens your direct invite link to connect peer-to-peer with zero server lag.

Can I install XO Arena as an app on PC, Android, or iOS?

Yes! XO Arena is a full Progressive Web App. On Android and PC Chrome/Edge, click the 'Install App' button. On iOS Safari, tap the Share icon (⎋) and select 'Add to Home Screen' (+) for a full native app experience with offline support.

Are there keyboard shortcuts for PC players?

Yes! PC players can use numeric keys 1 through 9 (or numpad 1-9) to select grid cells, R to request a rematch, Esc to exit to the lobby, and M to toggle sound.
