How Rules Work

1. 3-Mark Active Cap: Each player may have at most 3 marks on the grid.

2. First-In First-Out Decay: When you place your 4th piece, your 1st piece instantly clears.

3. Fading Indicator: The piece about to vanish is clearly highlighted with a glowing dashed warning ring.

4. Victory: Align 3 consecutive marks in any row, column, or diagonal to win!

Install XO Arena on iPhone / iPad

To install this game as a native fullscreen app without app store downloads:

  1. Tap the Share button (box with upward arrow) at the bottom of Safari.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add in the top right corner.
