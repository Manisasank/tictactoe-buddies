ARENA RULES & REGULATIONS

Official competitive guidelines for Infinity Tic-Tac-Toe, speed timers, bot regulations & matchmaking.

⚡ 1. The Dynamic Infinity Rule (FIFO)
  • 3-Mark Active Cap: Each player is restricted to a maximum of 3 marks on the grid.
  • First-In First-Out Decay: Placing your 4th mark instantly clears your oldest (1st) piece.
  • Vanish Warning Ring: The expiring piece pulses with an orange/coral dashed warning halo.
  • Zero Draws: Continuous cycling guarantees no stalemates. Connect 3 to win!
⏱️ 2. Match Regulations & Blitz Clock
  • Turn Succession: Tactician X moves first; turns alternate sequentially.
  • Blitz Speed Timer: Optional 5s speed or 10s standard countdown timer per turn.
  • Timeout Penalty: Failing to place a mark before the timer expires forfeits your turn.
🤖 3. AI Fair Play & Elo System
  • AI Tiers: Easy (Trainee bot), Medium (Sentinel), Hard (Unbeatable Minimax).
  • Starting Elo: All players begin at 1200 Elo. Win streaks boost your tier ranking.
  • Fair Play: Quitting or disconnecting during an active online match registers as a loss.
